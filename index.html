      All campaigns

      Results and average cost per result follow each campaign’s own goal (see Result type), so compare them campaign by campaign. Select a column heading to sort.

      All figures are lifetime totals, from each campaign’s start date to the time shown at the top. YouTube covers Google Ads Video campaigns only.

      Budget is a campaign’s total budget or, where the budget is set daily or on its ad sets or ad groups, those budgets added up over the campaign’s schedule. Totals count campaigns that are active or have spent.

      Reach counts each person once within a platform. Platforms can’t tell when the same person saw ads on more than one platform, so reach in the All view is the platforms’ figures added together. Google Ads doesn’t report reach for these campaigns.

      ROAS and purchase value appear once campaigns record purchases: through the Meta Pixel or Conversions API, the TikTok Pixel, or Google Ads conversion tracking (shown as conversion value).
