    Results and average cost per result follow each campaign’s own goal (see Result type), so compare them campaign by campaign. Select a column heading to sort.

    All figures are lifetime totals from Meta Ads Manager, from each campaign’s start date to the time shown at the top. Reach in the totals counts each account once, so it is lower than the campaigns’ reach added together.

    Budget is a campaign’s lifetime budget or, where the budget is set on its ad sets, those budgets added together. The total counts campaigns that are active or have spent; paused campaigns that never ran are left out.
